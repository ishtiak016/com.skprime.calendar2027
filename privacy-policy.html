Calendar 2027 Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Calendar 2027 is an offline calendar, notes and reminder application published by SK PRIME.

Data collection and sharing

SK PRIME does not receive your calendar entries or notes. The app uses Google AdMob to display ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP address (which may indicate approximate location), app interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Data sent by the SDK is encrypted in transit. Ad personalization and consent choices depend on your region and selections.

Data stored on your device

Calendar entries, notes, categories and preferences are stored locally on your device. Android may include this information in a device or cloud backup if you enable Android backup. That backup is controlled by your device and Google account settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses the permission to show reminders that you create. Reminder information is processed locally on your device.

Advertising and privacy choices

The app uses Google AdMob. Where required, a consent message is shown before ads are requested. You can review available choices from Settings > Ad Privacy Choices. You can also reset or delete your Android advertising ID from Android settings. For details about Google's processing, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Data deletion

You can delete individual entries in the app. You can remove all app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Backup copies are controlled through your Android backup settings.

Children

The app is a general productivity tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's features or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact email shown on the Calendar 2027 Google Play listing.
